# Original bus surface candidate

This workspace is isolated from production, the M02 UI and the frozen Swingby renderer. It was copied from the published hardware factory whose SHA-256 is `6597ad74baa296be57fc55f1161f85452bb67465805169eab180f446f4d663dd`. `public` and `node_modules` are read-only usage junctions; no source asset, package or catalogue is modified there.

The candidate adds original segmented insulation blankets with real recessed seams, L-shaped corner-rail end shoes, and two restrained flush service hatches and fasteners. The continuous original top cover is preserved. A small fixed chassis conduit stays outside the certified bay; it is decorative construction detail, not a claimed electrical connection. No external game geometry, vendor CAD, generated image, extra texture request or new physical specification is used.

`createBus(design, surfaceStyle)` accepts `classic` and `panelled-v1`. The isolated candidate defaults to `panelled-v1`; a one-argument default change or explicit `classic` rolls it back. Legacy designs return the unchanged legacy factory before any new-art branch. This is deliberately not a save-format, catalogue or simulation flag.

The exact outer dimensions, mount surfaces, service-bay limits, cover visibility tags, published mass/cost and authoritative collision/shadow descriptors remain unchanged. Added geometry is a cosmetic distribution inside the existing lumped bus mass/envelope, not a newly measured CAD mass model. Each instance owns its geometries and existing per-instance foil/accent materials. Shared factory materials and two protected atlas textures remain under the existing pool ownership; no new material family or atlas is introduced.

The final actual assembly capture measures 11,358→14,430 bus triangles (+3,072, below the 8,000 cap), 9→9 mesh/material batches and 3→3 texture references. The preceding optional top split used 300 additional triangles; it is absent from the final source. No new bitmap requests. The frozen classic and legacy geometry/material values compare numerically against the captured production factory fixture.

Files to review are `src/render/bus-surface-detail.ts` and the small `src/render/parts.ts` delta. Additional verification is in `tests/bus-surface-detail.test.ts`, backed by `tests/fixtures/parts-v5-baseline.ts` (only import paths adjusted from the captured factory).43 focused CPU tests and TypeScript pass. The nine new tests cover every vertex/envelope and free bay, both budgets, exact classic/legacy output, open-bay visibility, real seam/hatch surface separation and protected shared ownership.

Twenty actual assembly screenshots were captured with identical camera, Sun, exposure and design before/after: two bus profiles, normal/close, open/closed bay and large UI. The first visual review found the new white top-cover bevel intersecting the dark backing near its edges. Correcting that separation passed a ray test, but the small seam still appeared dotted at normal scale. The optional top split was therefore removed; the proven continuous original cover stays. Three native context restores followed by actual bus replacement/disposal passed with zero native or browser errors on the preceding cap-only variant. Those lifecycle checks were not repeated on the final continuous-lid source.

The final source passed all 43 focused CPU tests and TypeScript. A fresh two-image actual assembly comparison of the Pathfinder bus at the normal closed-bay camera passed in 4.6 seconds on Chrome 157.0.8103.52. The exact camera pose, Sun, exposure, design and three texture references were preserved. The final visual review confirms the dotted lid seam is absent, while the blanket joint, corner shoes and flush hatch remain legible. There were no page/HTTP/native errors; one existing shader compiler warning was retained. This is visual and functional evidence, not a performance measurement or a new final-source context-restoration claim.

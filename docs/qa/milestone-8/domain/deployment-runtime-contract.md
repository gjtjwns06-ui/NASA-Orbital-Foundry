# Commissioning domain contract

This is an isolated new model. No released simulation, hardware2, transfer or swingby arithmetic is edited. `research/legacy-dependency-manifest.json` pins all 19 copied pure dependencies. The domain exports are in `src/deployment/index.ts`; no DOM, renderer, Worker or storage side effects are imported.

`createDeploymentCraft()` and `createDeploymentPlan()` create the compact draft. The existing fixed-wing alternatives use `createDeploymentCraft('fixed')` and `createDeploymentPlan(true)`. `createDeploymentClearanceLesson(repaired)` returns a real passive cable-guide repair: the guide remains on pz at u=.484 and rotation0, while v changes from .59 to .70. The guide adds its authored0.15kg/3credits in either placement. `createDeploymentRecoveryLesson()` adds two real15W body panels and a late program; it receives no special energy, state or success rule.

`compileDeploymentCraft` returns immutable shared topology, ordinary hardware connections, mass/cost/fairing quantities, conservative drive certificates and design issues. Static intersections, internal bay fit, external attachment fit, model counts and every requested deploy/retract endpoint are validated. A formally known obstructed path blocks launch. Each actual motor start independently recertifies its actual partial pose; a newly obstructed or uncertain path safely stops at its certified prefix.

`createDeploymentRuntime(craft,plan,numerics)` is pure apart from an internal weak cache. `advanceDeployment(runtime,seconds,maxSteps)` adds bounded demand and mutates only committed canonical intervals. Zero or insufficient demand cannot start a motor, expend energy or emit a bit. Externally scheduled actions are preloaded, serialized on one actuator channel. Physical actions command only `stowed` or `deployed`; an arbitrary-angle editor scrub is a separate FK preview and cannot be installed as flight state.

`getDeploymentGeometry`, `getDeploymentTelemetry` and `getDeploymentResult` supply the actual state. Public geometry/telemetry values are detached from internal cached physics. The orbital position is the whole-craft point-mass COM in EarthMJ2000Eq, in km. The quaternion maps structural-body axes into that frame. Renderer structural-origin placement is orbit COM minus rotated geometry COM (metres converted to km), then the supplied local-to-body link poses are applied. Epoch is2026-11-15 00:00:00 TAI, elapsed SI seconds. Fixed educational Sun orientation, spherical Earth and authored rotating stations are not an ephemeris claim for that calendar instant.

The finite motor model separates release, powered trajectory, passive brake, waiting, mechanical latch verification and device-specific electrical/RF contacts. Contacts require a complete powered verification interval. A stopped pose retains its angle, rates and accumulated joules. This is a device-specific authored interlock; it is not a claim that all real solar panels require a latch before producing electricity. The generic ground-test kernel is `advanceMechanism(...,{allowClearanceStop:true,...})`; its explicitly supplied laboratory source is not a spacecraft battery or a mission-success path.

The receiver gets16Mbit of engineering diagnostics only after a real90W/60s stable-power test and actual downlink. Useful frames contain64kbit over1s. The radio uses the lowest supported catalog signal mode with enough coded capacity; Iris therefore uses131072bit/s, with unused capacity declared as framing/padding. Both instantaneous and conservative whole-frame margins use that supported RF rate. Refused admission emits no frame and consumes no packet ID. Admitted frames may still fail if EPS or operation availability changes; already transmitted complete frames keep their moving-station arrival time. No ground ACK knowledge is invented to delete stored data.

RF propagation uses the spacecraft COM as its point transmitter; metre-scale aperture parallax is omitted. Body occlusion uses each actual local aperture and its declared nine-ray quadrature. Array dimensions and the two-axis Gaussian beam are authored. Ideal frequency tracking, fixed noise/gain/system-loss assumptions and the simplified orbit/eclipse model are exposed limitations, not a claim to implement a flight communications stack.

The attitude controller is a bounded midpoint PD torque update with RK4 quaternion integration through actual articulated geometry. Torque and wheel-momentum caps remain the unchanged selected ADCS values. This integrates a declared ideal feedback model; it does not resolve wheel rotor spin efficiency or high-frequency structural flex. The drive certificate separately bounds inertial mechanical work for every permitted joint angle. See `drive-feasibility.md` for the derivation and limits.

Save format is `orbital-foundry-deployment`, version1, with explicit model/catalog/scenario identity and numerics. `createDeploymentReplay(text)` validates bounded UTF-8/schema/identity, reconstructs from original inputs and compares the entire physical checkpoint. It never installs imported angles, energy, latch, momentum or receipts. Replay is detached, chunked and cancellable. Editor ghosts and reduced-motion settings are outside the authoritative save. Released save formats are not upgraded or reinterpreted.

Fatal power loss pins `fatalAtSeconds` and permanently retains `status:'failed'` and `reason:'POWER_LOSS'`. `advanceDeployment` returns at this newly committed cutoff. `hasDeploymentPendingDrain(runtime)` then permits a separately labelled receiver phase, bounded by `receiverDrainUntilSeconds` and `getDeploymentAdvanceLimit(runtime)`. Already emitted full frames can still reach Earth; no new powered work, solar credit or RF emission occurs. Partial tails are resolved without useful-data credit. `getDeploymentResult` exposes cutoff, later evaluation time and `pendingReceiverDrain` separately, so receipt of all diagnostic bits after death cannot become mission success. See `terminal-receiver-drain-proposal.md` for the conservative failing-interval and unpowered-motion assumptions.

Acceptance is recorded separately with exact source hashes. Earlier successful or rejected candidate artifacts remain in `docs/qa`; they are not automatically proof for a later numerical candidate. Final UI, art, Worker and deployment gates belong to the integrating parent and renderer owner.

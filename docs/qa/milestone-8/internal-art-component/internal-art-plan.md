# Bounded commissioning internal hardware art

This isolated candidate inherits the accepted deployment scene and subsequent viewport/grab bug fixes. Only a new internal-detail module and the electronics branch in `deployment-link-meshes.ts` are intended for integration. `deployment-link-meshes-baseline.ts` preserves the accepted original output for matched comparisons and external byte-signature tests. Root product/UI/domain files are untouched.

The original internal art will distinguish sealed battery packs, shielded RF cards, three orthogonal ADCS wheel housings, recorder memory banks, and mechanism-controller drivers. It is an educational appearance convention inside authoritative `LocalBox` solids, not manufacturer CAD, a new mass distribution or a connection solver. Short conductors stay within a board/enclosure; no cable spans undeclared free bay space. Blank identification plates and sparse original geometric marks avoid claiming unreadable vendor labels.

The implementation reuses per-view PBR materials, adds no texture/network request, and merges details by material per rigid link. Target: at most four material batches per internal link and at most6,000 additional triangles across the default five internal components. All vertices must remain within supplied boxes, including rotated-box fixtures. Topology, envelopes, mass/FK/clearance data and external solar/RF output must remain unchanged. Picking remains the same rigid link / part identity.

Meaningful gates: bounded geometry for all supported internal families, model distinctions, unchanged external geometry signatures, unchanged domain JSON, owned actual/preview material/geometry disposal, measured triangles/batches, and same-camera whole/open-bay plus focused screenshots at product-relevant resolutions. A good close-up alone is insufficient; the whole-craft view must also remain coherent. This candidate has no acceptance or graphics-parity claim before those checks.

Earth near-view framing/texture proposals are a separate research note. This art pass does not change the live camera, Earth position/radius, orbit state, or asset downloads.
